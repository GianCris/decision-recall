# Decision Recall — Decision Log

## D-001 — Concept phase closed
The conceptual phase is closed. New mechanisms are added only when evidence or a critical experimental flaw justifies them.

## D-002 — Evidence before Fleet infrastructure
Decision Recall must demonstrate its core mechanism before investing deeply in Fortified Enterprise Fleet infrastructure.

## D-003 — Discovery and Recovery evaluated separately
Discovery and Recovery will be experimentally isolated so that improvement in one cannot hide weakness in the other.

## D-004 — No mechanism is protected
Semantic Decision Lineage, Decision Sensitivity, Justification Trace, Reliance Load, counterfactual replay and every architectural hypothesis may be removed if evidence does not support them.

## D-005 — FLEET-1 v0.1 frozen before results
The FLEET-1 experimental protocol is frozen before the first meaningful experimental results. Changes require a documented methodological reason and must not be made to improve observed scores retroactively.
